    <h2 class="fold-heading">Recording the brain is easy. Proving what a drug does to it is not.</h2>
    <p class="fold-lead">If you run trials or care for patients with neurological conditions, this may sound familiar:</p>

    <ul class="problem-list">
      <li><strong>Questionnaires that miss what the drug does.</strong> Patient-reported scales are noisy and open to placebo and expectation effects. You need objective measures.</li>
      <li><strong>Signals too subtle to see by eye.</strong> Early changes in brain rhythms, like the slowing seen in cognitive decline, are easy to miss without careful quantification.</li>
      <li><strong>Findings that vanish in the next cohort.</strong> An EEG marker looks promising in one study and fails to replicate in the next.</li>
      <li><strong>Results you can’t defend.</strong> A model gives a number, but nobody can explain it to clinicians, reviewers or regulators.</li>
    </ul>

    <p class="fold-lead"><strong>That’s where I come in.</strong> I measure what’s really in the recording, make sure it holds up, and explain every step, so you can show what your drug does to the brain.</p>

    <h2 class="fold-heading">How we work together</h2>
    <p class="fold-lead">I’m a neuroscientist who works with EEG recordings from real patients. Here’s how I turn your recordings into evidence you can act on.</p>

    <ol class="steps">
      <li><strong>Understand your question.</strong> We start with your trial, your patients and your decision. What do you need to prove, and to whom?</li>
      <li><strong>Design the analysis.</strong> I turn that question into a clear analysis plan: endpoints, measures and success criteria, agreed before any data is touched.</li>
      <li><strong>Extract the evidence.</strong> I clean and analyze your EEG with methods that go beyond standard measures, to find the signal that matters for your question.</li>
      <li><strong>Stress-test the results.</strong> Every finding is checked for robustness and, where possible, replicated in independent data, before it reaches your team.</li>
      <li><strong>Deliver and hand over.</strong> You receive a clear report, figures ready for partners and regulators, and documented code your team can run and extend.</li>
